Where product strategy meets delivery and engineering.

I turn ambitious strategy into outcome-based roadmaps that deliver measurable business results. Across 18 years in digital technology - at both high-growth startups and large enterprises - I've owned product portfolios end-to-end: from establishing digital strategy to building 0-to-1 products and scaling them to millions of users, leading complex platform migrations, and modernizing mission-critical web and mobile applications through iterative, data-driven optimization.

I have led leaders - managing Product, Delivery, and Engineering Leads, each running their own teams - while owning budgets of up to $6M and owning the full product and delivery operating model. My approach is outcome-driven: I set OKR-based roadmaps that connect product decisions directly to business results, create clear accountability across product, engineering, and design, and make sure the team is always building the right things in the right order.

What makes the work stick is how it gets done. I build trust at every level, translating business intent into technical direction, aligning stakeholders across complex organizations, and keeping teams focused through ambiguity. I'm known for being direct, low-ego, and the kind of leader both executives and delivery teams want in the room.

I build high-performing, accountable teams, sustaining top engagement while driving conversion, adoption, and cost efficiency across the business, and helping people grow into roles they hadn't yet imagined for themselves.

Problems I solve

Where I create the most value.

The challenges I tackle, and the outcomes I deliver against them.

01
"Our product isn't driving the outcomes that matter."
I connect product decisions to business results by setting OKR-driven, outcome-based roadmaps that give teams a clear north star. Work that doubled conversion, tripled self-serve adoption, and lifted mid-funnel progression from 51% to 61%.
02
"We're building the wrong things."
I bring rigour to prioritization, cutting through noise with data-driven frameworks that connect every item on the roadmap to a business outcome. The result: teams that ship what moves the needle, not just what's loudest in the room.
03
"Product and engineering aren't aligned."
I close that gap by translating business intent into technical direction, creating the operating rhythm both sides can commit to, and managing complex dependencies across multi-team programs. Delivered results include cutting page load from 15s to 6s and shipping complex migrations on time and on budget.
04
"AI feels like hype, not impact."
I make AI practical, not theoretical, embedding it into how teams actually work: synthesizing workshop outcomes, translating business requirements into technical specs, and shipping real AI-driven product capabilities that customers use.
05
"Nobody can get stakeholders aligned."
I earn trust across business, product, and engineering, translating between each so decisions stick. That alignment has delivered results including tripling self-serve adoption, $327K in annual ops savings, and $2.5M in capital cost reductions.
06
"We're scaling and the model is breaking."
I've built and run director-level product organizations, leading a leadership team of a Product Lead, Delivery Lead, and Engineering Lead - each with their own teams beneath them - while owning product portfolios up to $6M and designing the operating structures that keep high-stakes programs on track through growth and change.
